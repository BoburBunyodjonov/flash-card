## Competitor landscape

---

### 🟢 Duolingo — The 800-lb Gorilla
**Market position:** #1 language learning app globally, 500M+ users
**What they do brilliantly:**
- Streak system is addictive — users literally pay $13/month just to "streak freeze"
- Hearts system creates urgency and FOMO (lose hearts → feel bad → don't quit)
- XP league system (Bronze→Diamond) drives weekly competition
- Bite-sized 5-minute lessons perfectly fit mobile attention spans
- Localized mascot (Duo the owl) creates emotional connection
- Push notifications are aggressive but personalized

**What they do poorly:**
- Too game-y — users can finish lessons without actually retaining words
- No spaced repetition by default (Duolingo Max has it but it's expensive)
- English from Uzbek is a 2nd class product — poor Uzbek translations
- $6.99/month feels expensive for Uzbek market
- No Telegram integration — requires separate app download

**Ideas to steal from Duolingo:**
- League system (Bronze/Silver/Gold/Diamond) based on weekly XP — drives re-engagement
- "Streak freeze" as a premium perk — "Miss a day? Use your streak shield"
- Weekly XP competition — top 10 in your league go up, bottom 5 go down
- Celebratory animations on milestone achievements (100 words, 30-day streak)

---

### 🟡 Anki — The Power User Choice
**Market position:** Niche but extremely loyal, loved by medical students and language learners
**What they do brilliantly:**
- Pure SM-2 spaced repetition — scientifically optimal
- Completely customizable — users create their own decks
- Massive shared deck library (150M+ user-created cards)
- Desktop app is free, cross-platform sync via AnkiWeb

**What they do poorly:**
- Terrible UI/UX — looks like 2005
- Mobile app costs $25 (iOS) — huge barrier
- No social features whatsoever
- Extremely steep learning curve
- No Telegram integration

**Ideas to steal from Anki:**
- Let users share their decks publicly — creates community content
- "Cram mode" — review all cards regardless of schedule (exam prep)
- Deck import/export — power users love this

---

### 🔵 Quizlet — The Student Favorite
**Market position:** Dominant in US high schools/universities, 500M+ study sets
**What they do brilliantly:**
- Multiple study modes: Flashcards, Learn, Write, Spell, Test, Match
- Teacher tools — creates organic school adoption
- "Match" game (fastest match = leaderboard) is extremely engaging
- AI features (Quizlet Q-Chat) for explanations

**What they do poorly:**
- Became paywalled — free tier is now nearly useless
- No Telegram integration
- Not localized for Uzbek market at all
- Heavy web-first, mobile is secondary

**Ideas to steal from Quizlet:**
- Match game — tap matching pairs against timer → leaderboard
- Multiple study modes beyond just flashcards
- "Test yourself" mode — fill in the blank from the word list

---

### 🟠 Memrise — The Fun One
**Market position:** Mid-tier, strong in European languages
**What they do brilliantly:**
- "Mems" — user-created memory tricks (mnemonics) for words
- Native speaker video clips for pronunciation
- Immersion videos — real content (movies, YouTube) with word highlighting
- "Classic review" vs "Speed review" modes

**What they do poorly:**
- Inconsistent quality across languages
- Expensive premium ($8.49/month)
- Not available in Uzbek at all

**Ideas to steal from Memrise:**
- User-submitted memory tricks — "How I remember this word"
- Speed review mode — fast-paced version for warm-up

---

### 🇺🇿 Local Uzbek competitors

**EnglishUz (Telegram bot):**
- Simple bot that sends word of the day
- 50K+ subscribers but no retention mechanism
- No spaced repetition, no gamification
- **Our advantage:** We have SM-2, streaks, social features

**IELTS Uzbekistan channels:**
- Mostly static content, no interactive learning
- 100K+ followers hungry for English learning
- **Opportunity:** Partner with these channels for distribution

**Conclusion:** There is NO strong interactive English vocabulary app specifically for Uzbek users. This is our moat.

## WordSwipe's unique advantages

1. **Telegram-native** — zero friction, no app install needed
2. **Uzbek-first** — translations, examples in Uzbek context
3. **TikTok mechanics** — more engaging than traditional flashcards
4. **Local pricing** — 25,000 UZS vs $7/month feels 10x more accessible
5. **SM-2 algorithm** — better retention than Duolingo's approach
