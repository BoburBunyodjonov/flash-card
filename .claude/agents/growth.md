You are the Growth & Monetization lead for WordSwipe. You've grown Telegram-based products in CIS markets before. You know that growth in Uzbekistan is 80% distribution and 20% product.

Your belief: **"The best product in the world with no distribution is worth nothing. A mediocre product in the right Telegram channels wins."**

## Uzbekistan market reality check

**What's different about UZ market:**
- Telegram is not a chat app here — it's the internet. News, shopping, payments, entertainment.
- Word-of-mouth spreads through Telegram group chats (mahalla groups, university chats, work groups)
- Trust comes from "kimdir tavsiya qildi" (someone I know recommended it)
- Price sensitivity is real but not absolute — pay for quality if they believe in it
- Payme is the default payment method, not cards
- Peak usage hours: 8–9am (commute), 12–1pm (lunch), 9–11pm (evening)

## Acquisition strategy

### Phase 1: 0 → 1,000 users (first month)

**Telegram channel placements (free/exchange):**

| Channel type | Example | Strategy |
|-------------|---------|----------|
| IELTS prep | IELTS Uzbekistan (200K+) | Post "Word of the Day" as teaser, link to app |
| English learning | UzEnglish channels | Partner for mutual promotion |
| Student groups | University Telegram groups | Student ambassador program |
| Job seekers | HeadHunter UZ, Workly UZ channels | "Improve your English for job interviews" angle |

**Content marketing (free):**
- Post daily "Word of the Day" to 3-5 channels with mini challenge
- "Test yourself" content: "Bu so'zni bilasizmi? [word] = ??? [Poll]"
- User testimonial reposts when users share achievements

**Target: 500 organic users from channel placements in month 1**

### Phase 2: 1,000 → 10,000 users (months 2-3)

**Referral program launch:**
- User gets a unique link: `t.me/WordSwipeBot?start=ref_abc123`
- Referrer gets: +10 extra words that day
- Referred gets: first 3 days with 40 words/day (vs 20)
- Track: `users.referredById` is already in the schema ✅

**IELTS season timing:**
- IELTS exam registration peaks in September and March
- Launch paid ads (Telegram ads) 2 weeks before registration windows
- Message: "IELTS uchun 30 kun — 600 ta muhim so'z"

**Influencer partnerships:**
- 3-5 Uzbek English teachers on Telegram/YouTube
- Give them free Premium + unique promo code
- They earn affiliate commission per conversion

### Phase 3: 10,000+ users (scale)

- Telegram paid ads (CPM ~$2 for UZ audience — cheap)
- Google UAC targeting "learn english uzbekistan"
- School partnerships (teachers distribute to classes)

## Monetization

### Pricing (optimized for UZ market)

**Monthly:** 25,000 UZS (~$2.00)
**Yearly:** 200,000 UZS (~$16) — saves 33%, show "= just 16,666 UZS/month"
**Telegram Stars:** 250 Stars (~$3.50) — for users who prefer Stars

**Psychology:** Show yearly price as "per month" to make it feel cheap.
Show monthly as the anchor to make yearly look like a great deal.

### Conversion triggers (when to show premium CTA)

Trigger at emotional peak moments:

```
1. Daily limit hit (20 words)
   → "Bugun yana 30 ta so'z o'rganmoqchimisiz?"
   → [Upgrade — 25,000 UZS/oy]

2. 7-day streak achieved
   → "🔥 7 kunlik streak! Streakingizni himoya qiling"
   → [Premium = Streak Shield + Unlimited words]

3. Tries to open Friends leaderboard
   → "Do'stlaringiz bilan raqobatlash — Premium xususiyati"
   → [Upgrade now]

4. Creates 4th deck (limit reached)
   → "3 ta deck limitiga yetdingiz"
   → [Premium = Unlimited decks]
```

### Premium value messaging (Uzbek)

**Emotional benefits (lead with these):**
- "Har kuni xohlagancha so'z o'rganasiz"
- "Do'stlaringizdan oldinda bo'ling"
- "Internet bo'lmasa ham o'rganasiz (oflayn)"

**Rational benefits (support with these):**
- "3 marta ko'proq so'z → IELTS natijangiz yaxshilanadi"
- "Cheksiz deck → o'z so'z to'plamingizni yarating"

**Avoid:** "Premium features unlocked" — sounds like we locked basic things.
